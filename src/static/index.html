Frequently Asked Questions

Enter your school email address and select an activity from the dropdown list in the Sign Up for an Activity section, then click Sign Up.

There is no hard limit on the number of activities you can join, but be mindful of scheduling conflicts and each activity's capacity.

If an activity has no spots remaining you will not be able to sign up. Check back later — a spot may open if another student unregisters.

Find your email in the participant list of the activity card and click the ❌ button next to your name. Your spot will be freed immediately.

Each activity card shows its schedule. Activities typically run after school hours. Check the schedule listed on each card for exact days and times.

Reach out to the activity coordinator listed on the school's main notice board, or speak to your homeroom teacher for guidance on joining any club or team.
